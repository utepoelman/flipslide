How to Play
FlipSlide: The Art of Isometric Interference

Objective

Pieces stand on the points where the gridlines cross. Each player starts with all of their pieces stacked on their starting corner and must move every piece to the diagonal opposite corner. The first player to get all of their pieces onto their target point wins the round.

  • Blue: bottom-left corner → top-right corner.
  • Red: bottom-right corner → top-left corner.

Scoring

  • Round Winner: Scores points based on how far the loser's pieces are from their target.
  • Calculation: Points = for each of the loser's pieces, (distance X + distance Y), added together. This is the "City Block" distance.

Movement Cards

Each piece has two arrows. Blue lines run up and down, red lines run left and right. A card always works along the line of its own colour.

  • SLIDE: Translate a piece 1 to 5 steps along a line of the card's colour, in the direction its arrow on that line is pointing. Blue cards slide up or down; red cards slide left or right.
  • TURN: Rotate a piece 90° clockwise or anti-clockwise about its own centre.
  • FLIP: Reflect a piece in the line of the card's colour that passes through it. The piece stays on its point but its arrows become a mirror image. Blue cards flip it in the vertical line, red cards in the horizontal line.
  • FLIP & SLIDE: A glide reflection. Reflect the piece in the line of the card's colour, then slide it 1 to 5 steps along that same line in the direction of its arrow.

Rules

  • Choosing a piece: If you have more than one piece, click one of your pieces to choose it (click it again to cycle through pieces stacked on the same point), then pick a card. The chosen piece pulsates smoothly on its turn.
  • Stacking: Your own pieces may share or overlap the same points.
  • Collision: If a piece lands on another player's piece, even just partially overlapping it, that piece is sent back to its start.
  • Safe Zones: There are five grey safe zones: the centre and four surrounding zones. A piece standing on the centre of a grey zone cannot be sent back, and you cannot land on or overlap it. A piece that has reached its target is also safe.
  • Arriving: A piece that lands exactly on its target point stays there for good.
  • Who starts: The player to go first is chosen at random at the start of each round.
  • Confirming Moves: Click a card to preview. Click the card again (or the preview piece on the board) to confirm.
  • Stuck? Use the "Replace All" button to skip your turn and get a new hand.

Objective

Pieces stand on the points where the coloured lines cross. Each player starts with all of their pieces stacked on their starting corner and must move every piece to the opposite corner. The first player to get all of their pieces onto their target point wins the round. Each player's two corners are shaded in their colour and are joined by a line of that colour.

  • Blue: bottom-right corner → top-left corner.
  • Red: bottom-left corner → top-right corner.
  • Green (3-player games): top corner → bottom corner.

Scoring

  • Round Winner: Scores points for each opponent, based on how far that opponent's pieces are from their target.
  • Calculation: Points = for each of the opponent's pieces, the number of steps along the lines to its target, added together.

The Three Directions

The lines come in three colours, and every card has one of those colours. Each piece has three arrows, one lying along each colour of line. A card always works along the line of its own colour, using the arrow that lies on that line:

  • Blue lines run top-left ↔ bottom-right.
  • Green lines run straight up ↔ down.
  • Red lines run bottom-left ↔ top-right.

The arrows turn with the piece, so turning changes which way each colour of card moves it.

Movement Cards

  • SLIDE: Translate a piece 1 to 5 steps along the line of that colour, in the direction its arrow on that line is pointing.
  • TURN: Rotate a piece 60° or 120°, clockwise or anti-clockwise, about its own centre.
  • FLIP: Reflect a piece in the line of that colour that passes through it. The piece stays on its point but is turned over, so its arrows become a mirror image.
  • FLIP & SLIDE: A glide reflection. Reflect the piece in the line of that colour, then slide it 1 to 5 steps along that same line in the direction of its arrow.

Rules

  • Choosing a piece: If you have more than one piece, click one of your pieces to choose it (click it again to cycle through pieces stacked on the same point), then pick a card. The chosen piece pulsates smoothly on its turn.
  • Stacking: Your own pieces may share or overlap the same points.
  • Collision: If a piece lands on another player's piece, even just partially overlapping it, that piece is sent back to its start.
  • Safe Zones: There are seven grey safe zones: the centre and six more positioned around it. A piece standing on the centre of a grey zone cannot be sent back, and you cannot land on or overlap it. A piece that has reached its target is also safe.
  • Arriving: A piece that lands exactly on its target point stays there for good.
  • Who starts: The player to go first is chosen at random at the start of each round.
  • Confirming Moves: Click a card to preview. Click the card again (or the preview piece on the board) to confirm.
  • Stuck? Use the "Replace All" button to skip your turn and get a new hand.
