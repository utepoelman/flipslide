How to Play
FlipSlide: The Art of Isometric Interference

Objective

Pieces stand on gridline intersections. Move every piece to the opposite corner. First player to reach their target point with all pieces wins the round.

  • Blue: bottom-left → top-right.
  • Red: bottom-right → top-left.

Movement Cards

  • SLIDE: Translate a piece 1 to 5 steps along a line of card's color.
  • TURN: Rotate a piece 90° clockwise or anti-clockwise.
  • FLIP: Reflect a piece in the line of the card's color.
  • FLIP & SLIDE: Reflect the piece, then slide it 1 to 5 steps.

Rules

  • Selecting Pieces: Click any of your pieces on the board to select it.
  • Collision: Landing on an opponent sends their piece back to start.
  • Safe Zones: Grey zones protect pieces from collision.

Objective

Pieces stand on hexagonal grid points. Move all pieces to the opposite corner to win.

  • Blue: bottom-right → top-left.
  • Red: bottom-left → top-right.
  • Green: top → bottom.
